Eight per room, chosen from the real photo and written here before any generation. Positions are described on the photo as shot (portrait, camera looking down the length of the room toward the hallway). Nothing a staging tool might legitimately move is used: no rugs, plants, stool, lamp or drying rack.

Scope: living room only. The dining nook and bedroom are not run in this version; their landmark lists are deferred (see `COVERAGE.md`).
